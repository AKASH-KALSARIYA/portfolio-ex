Philosophy

I believe restraint is the mark of a well-considered site. Fewer effects, more intention — every line of code serves the reading experience, every animation serves the rhythm. Elegance emerges from precise details, not their accumulation.

02 / 06


I work solo, end-to-end, on a small number of projects per year. Each site is conceived as a precise object: performance, readability, typographic detail, animation in service of the message. No imported systems, no UI kits — every line is written for the project at hand.
